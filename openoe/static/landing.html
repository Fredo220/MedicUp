How it works

From a few seeds to a reading list.

You bring the papers you trust. MedicUp builds the neighbourhood around them and ranks it, so you see the whole field without losing the thread.

Try it with the example seeds
01

Resolve your seeds

DOIs, doi.org links, PMIDs or titles are matched against OpenAlex. Ambiguous titles ask you to pick.

02

Expand

References and citing works of every seed, a literature search on your question, and a Tavily scholarly-web search.

03

Rank, out loud

Graph, semantic and optional LLM-judge signals combine into a 1–100% score with adjustable weights.

04

Decide & export

Accept or reject, see retraction flags, and export Markdown, BibTeX or RIS.

Transparency

Every result explains itself.

Each paper shows which seed it cites or is cited by, what it found through search or the web, and how every signal adds up to its score. Supporting web sources and a figure from the paper sit right next to it.

Evaluation

Measured on your own seeds.

MedicUp hides each seed in turn, ranks the field with the others, and records where the hidden paper lands for every channel. Recall@5, @10 and @20 come straight from that table, so you can see when the hybrid ranking beats plain keyword search.
