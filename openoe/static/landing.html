01 — Why

Every year, more than a million biomedical papers are published. The one that changes your answer is rarely in the first page of a keyword search. It sits two citations away from the work you already know.

Connected

Literature behaves like tissue.

Step 01 · Seed

Begin with what you trust.

Paste DOIs, PubMed IDs or titles. Each one is resolved against OpenAlex and checked before anything runs.

Step 02 · Expand

Look closer, then wider.

The papers your seeds cite, the papers that cite them, a keyword search and a scholarly web search — pooled and de-duplicated.

Step 03 · Rank

See why each paper is here.

Graph links to your seeds, semantic closeness to your question, and an optional expert judge combine into one score from 1–100%.

Step 04 · Read

Decide, with the context attached.

Web context, supporting sources and figures beside every paper. Retractions are flagged. Export to BibTeX or RIS.
